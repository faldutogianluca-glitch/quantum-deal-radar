## 3. What works

1. **LinkedIn, Gianluca's personal text posts that tease the newsletter**: median 510 impressions, best 1,974 and 1,739. Plan content since 13/10 (images, videos): median 96. Same account, 5x gap.
2. **The winning formula on LinkedIn**: first person, one scene, one exact number in the first line ("La casa aveva due ascensori", "Chi ha scelto 850?", "500.000 nell'annuncio, 465.000 nei conti"), posted on a Monday morning.
3. **TikTok, videos of 55 seconds or more with a face and money at stake**: 7 of 22 long videos broke 400 views; 0 of 33 short ones did. The best topics are Milan market data with a contrarian hook (median 496) and the deposit pill (724, 7 shares).
4. **Storytelling on YouTube**: the Bollate story is the best Short (209 views against 0-76 for the others).
5. **Shares are the breakout signal**: the videos that escaped the floor are the ones that got shared (caparra 7, inflation 5).

## 4. What does not work

1. **Volume without quality**: on TikTok, 11 videos in Apr-Aug had a median of 455 views; 53 in Sep-Oct had a median of 270. Five times the posts, -40% per post. (5 of the April-May videos were posted from the personal account @gianfald; on @quantumre alone the Apr-Aug median is about 475, so the conclusion holds.)
2. **Silent 16-second videos**: TikTok median 273, 0.2 likes each. Facebook 0-4 views. Instagram 3-34 people.
3. **Brand promos** ("Tutti vedono un problema...", "Mandi il dossier..."): median 264 on TikTok, 0 shares. On LinkedIn, plan videos get 47-115 impressions.
4. **Static images and carousels on Instagram/Facebook**: 0-4 people reached. With 9 and 241 followers there is no one to show them to.
5. **Off-brand content on the brand account**: on @quantumre TikTok there are 17 personal videos (Rome, beaches, quotes) and 10 ASMR videos, including English-language ASMR from another project ("Two new videos every day"). They confuse TikTok about who the account is for.
6. **Off-topic LinkedIn posts** (physical AI, Steve Jobs): fine for reach, but they do not build the real-estate authority.

## 5. Compliance risks found in published posts

- LinkedIn 19/10: compares a Milan rental yield with the 10-year BTP. The rules forbid comparing property and government bonds.
- LinkedIn 21/9: mentions a club deal partner and ROI/IRR thresholds.
- Older TikTok captions use #clubdeal and #investimentiimmobiliari.

## 6. Decisions proposed

**Stop**: silent 16-second videos; static images and carousels on Instagram/Facebook; brand promos; personal and ASMR posts on @quantumre; investment language and hashtags.

**Keep**: personal text posts on LinkedIn (Monday 09:30-11:00); avatar videos of 55-65 s with a face, a hook on frame 0 and subtitles (pills, Impegni, book); market data with a contrarian hook.

**Start**: UTM links per network on valutazione.quantumre.it, to measure leads instead of views; the newsletter link in the TikTok bio (Linktree); one "money at stake" pill per week on TikTok; Instagram Trial Reels to reach non-followers.

**Rhythm (per week)**: LinkedIn 3 posts (2 personal text, 1 carousel or pill). TikTok 3 face videos. Instagram/Facebook/YouTube: the same 3 videos, nothing else.

## 7. KPIs

- Leads from valutazione.quantumre.it per network (UTM).
- TikTok: share of videos above 400 views, shares per video, new followers per week.
- LinkedIn: impressions per post, comments, newsletter subscribers.
- Instagram: non-follower reach from Trial Reels.
