# MiRT Radar – HeyGen video plan: "Storie dal Tour"
Avatar and voice: Andrea Bertagnolio (co-founder of Creattiva and co-owner of Milano Retail Tour, authorised).
Goal: promote the MiRT Radar 2027 (free PDF on /radar/) and the funnel behind it, using real stories from Milano Retail Tour.

## 1. Strategy in one paragraph
Series A (already written, `copioni-andrea.md`) explains the trend of the week. Series B, below, does what only Milano Retail Tour can do: Andrea tells a real story from 14 years of tours with IKEA, Luxottica, L'Oréal, Leroy Merlin, Rolex, Timberland and others, draws one lesson for a specialist store, and points to the Radar. Stories make people trust the brand; the Radar turns that trust into an email address; the Diagnosi turns the email into a conversation.

## 3. Calendar
- **Series A** (trend of the week): Thursdays 12:00, 29/10 → 17/12 (see `copioni-andrea.md`).
- **Series B** (Storie dal Tour): Tuesdays 12:00, 27/10 → 15/12, eight videos.
- **Launch video** (V0): the day /radar/ goes online.
- **Dependency:** every video points to /radar/. If the page is not online by 27/10, publish B1 and B2 with the Diagnosi call to action instead (link to /contatti/).
- Channels for every video: YouTube Short, Instagram Reel, Facebook Reel, and LinkedIn as native video. TikTok when the account is connected.

## 4. Format (same for every Series B video)
- 35–45 seconds, vertical 9:16, 1080×1920.
- 0–3 s hook on screen and spoken; 3–25 s the story; 25–35 s the lesson; last 5 s the call to action.
- Andrea framed chest-up, light grey background (#F6F6F7), or full-screen B-roll with Andrea in a corner during the story.
- B-roll: tour photos in `brand-kit/immagini/tour-1..4.jpg`, the Milan map background, the Radar cover `sito/download/mirt-radar-2027-anteprima.jpg`. Never use a client's logo or store photos without permission.
- Captions burned in (many people watch with the sound off), Merriweather, graphite #3D3C3F on white.
- Music: soft, low, no lyrics.

## 5. Production checklist in HeyGen (per video)
1. Avatar: Andrea Bertagnolio (custom). Voice: Andrea (cloned). Language: Italian.
2. Paste the spoken text exactly; numbers are already written so the voice reads them well.
3. Add the hook as a title in the first 3 seconds.
4. Turn on captions; style: Merriweather, graphite on white.
5. Add the line «Video realizzato con avatar AI di Andrea Bertagnolio» at the bottom for the whole video.
6. Export MP4 1080×1920, file name as above, into the MiRT Drive folder.
7. I schedule it on Metricool (YouTube Short with AI flag, Instagram and Facebook Reel with AI flag, LinkedIn video).

## 6. What to measure
- Views at 3 seconds (does the hook work?) and average watch time.
- Clicks to /radar/ with `utm_source=video`.
- Radar downloads and Diagnosi requests per week.
After four videos, keep the two hooks that worked best as the template for the rest.
