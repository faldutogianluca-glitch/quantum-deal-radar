Five trends to put on your 2027 agenda

14 December 2026 · MiRT Radar · Retail radar

Over eight weeks the MiRT Radar has looked at how specialist retail is changing: AI that recommends and buys, the augmented sales assistant, the new rules, Milan’s flagships, second-hand. Here is the summary in five trends, each with a month to put it on your agenda. You do not need to do them all: you need to choose where to start.

1. Getting found by AI (January)

In Italy, between August 2025 and May 2026, product discovery through traditional search fell by 15%, while discovery through AI assistants and new channels grew by 38% (Salesforce). 34% of Italians have already used an AI assistant to shop, 52% among Gen Z (Adyen). One of Mintel’s 2027 predictions says it all in its title: “The Recommendation Engine Is the New Brand”.

The move: fix your Google business profile, with category, opening hours, services and brands, and every month ask ChatGPT and Gemini where to buy what you sell.

2. Data and catalogue in order (February)

Only 42% of Italian companies have fully unified customer data, 44% do not sync inventory in real time and 37% have inconsistent prices across channels (Salesforce). And that is exactly what AI agents shopping for customers read: product, price, availability.

The move: one price list and one customer database, with privacy consent recorded. Start with one category.

3. The augmented, trained sales assistant (March)

87% of retail revenue still runs through the physical store, and equipping store staff is among retailers’ top technology priorities (IHL Group). Meanwhile the AI Act, as amended by the Digital Omnibus, asks companies using AI to support staff training.

The move: collect the twenty most frequent questions at the counter with the owner’s answer, and organise an hour of basic AI training for the whole team.

4. The store as a service and a fixed date (spring)

78.9% of Italian pharmacies offer tests and rapid checks (Federfarma). In Milan, Hoka has a weekly run club, L’Ottocento a material library. For more than 4 in 10 Gen Z and millennial shoppers, in-store experience weighs on their choice of retailer (ICSC and McKinsey). Mintel calls it “Participation Replaces Promotion”.

The move: a bookable service, or a fixed monthly appointment, always on the same day.

5. Trust: genuine reviews and guaranteed second-hand (all year)

Since 7 April 2026, reviews in Italy count if they arrive within 30 days, from someone who actually used the service, with no discount in return (law 34/2026). 65% of Italians buy or sell second-hand (Ipsos Doxa for Subito). Another Mintel prediction for 2027: “Trust Is Earned in Public”.

The move: ask for the review within a few days of the purchase and reply to all of them. Then try trade-in for a voucher in one category.
