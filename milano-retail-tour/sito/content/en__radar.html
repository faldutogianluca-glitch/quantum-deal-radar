A taste: trend 01

Customers ask AI where to buy

In Italy, between August 2025 and May 2026, product discovery through traditional search fell by 15%; through AI assistants and new channels it grew by 38% (Salesforce).

34% of Italians have already used an AI assistant to shop; 52% among Gen Z (Adyen).

Example. An optician with a precise category, opening hours and services on their Google profile gets mentioned when someone asks for “an optician open on Saturday near me”; one with an empty profile does not.

  • The first move: fix your Google profile (category, December hours, services and brands) and every month ask ChatGPT and Gemini where to buy what you sell.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. We only ask for your name, email and type of store, to understand who reads the Radar. We do not sign you up for anything without your consent.

No. Most of the moves are not about AI but about the basics that make it useful: Google profile, catalogue, prices, the owner’s answers.

Yes. The examples are single stores, but the moves also work for chains and brands with their own stores.
